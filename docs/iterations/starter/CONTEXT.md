# Context

The place of this repository's agents: the minimum (MVP) for principle 3, "the agent knows its
place". Fill each section in a line or two, in the person's own words. A framework (Zachman, a
DAMA context diagram) can replace this later.

## Purpose (required, no default)

Why does this exist, and whom does it serve? Only the person can say; it cannot be backfilled.

## Personas

Who is involved? Not roles: people. The operator is always one.

## Boundaries

For each agent: what it is responsible for, what it may not do, who must be consulted, who must
be informed. In effect its row in a RACI chart.

## Inputs

What comes in?

## Outputs

What goes out?

## Process

How does work flow, from request to done?

## Ceremonies

What happens on a rhythm? Give each a day and time, not "weekly".

## Lexicon

Where the words live (a pointer, usually `LEXICON.md`), and which words already mean something
specific here.

## Tooling

What the agents work with: commands, tasks, scripts, services. Link `.devcontainer/` and the
Taskfile.
